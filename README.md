# Platform

Platform is a minimal mindfulness app designed for train journeys.  
It helps you slow down, regulate your breathing, and stay calm while travelling.

## Idea

Commuting can be overstimulating. Platform creates a small mental pause during your journey by guiding you through simple breathing prompts at your own pace.

No chaos.
No clutter.
Just space to breathe.

## Features

- simple onboarding flow
- calm, minimal interface
- light and dark mode support
- breathing prompts designed for journeys of any duration

## How it works

On first launch, users are welcomed with a short introduction explaining the purpose of the app.

After onboarding:
- follow the on-screen prompts  
- breathe naturally  
- focus on slowing your rhythm  
- use it for as little or as long as your journey lasts  

## Tech

- SwiftUI
- @AppStorage for onboarding state
- Adaptive colour scheme support
